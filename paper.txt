A sequence-based oracle for scFv binding and developability
A. Montano Romero, [co-authors]
Abstract
We present an oracle that predicts binding and developability properties of single-chain variable fragments (scFvs) from sequence alone. Trained on a 30-member library characterised by a NanoBiT split-luciferase assay, our work achieves useful ranking performance on held-out clonal lineages. We release all code and data.
Introduction
Deep generative methods have transformed protein design over the past five years (Dauparas et al., 2022; Watson et al., 2022). Antibody-specific language models have followed (Olsen et al., 2022; Shuai et al., 2021). Property prediction, however, remains the bottleneck in most campaigns (Makowski et al., 2022).
Earlier work established that developability properties can be co-optimised with affinity (Harvey et al., 2022), though the trade-offs are library-specific.
Methods
Library design and expression
Constructs were expressed in Expi293 cells. Full construct maps are deposited under GenBank accession MZ457891.
Binding assay
Binding was measured with a NanoBiT split-luciferase reporter. Raw plate data are available in the repository.
Oracle training
Descriptors were computed per sequence and a random forest trained with lineage-disjoint cross-validation. Code is available at https://github.com/montano-lab/scfv-binding-oracle.
